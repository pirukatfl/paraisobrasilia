| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-09 | Tampering | Media upload | mitigate | `mimeTypes` jpeg/png/webp only (no SVG → no script in images), sharp re-encodes to WebP, 15 MB `upload.limits.fileSize` from 01-01 |

| T-01-10 | Information disclosure | EXIF/GPS in flyers | mitigate | sharp re-encode in imageSizes drops metadata by default; derivatives are what the site serves |
