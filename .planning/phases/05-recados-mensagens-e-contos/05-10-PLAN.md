| staff → report panel | sees a snapshot of private messages |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-07 | Elevation of privilege | blocked/suspended user keeps sending | mitigate | canSend on start and every send, both directions; int + e2e |
| T-05-09 | Information disclosure (LGPD) | staff reading conversations | mitigate | only the report snapshot (last 20 messages of the reported conversation); blocks/messages/conversations closed and hidden |
| T-05-28 | Information disclosure | reporter identity leaking to the reported person | mitigate | reports staff-only; UI never tells the reported person; block message neutral |
| T-05-29 | Denial of service | report flooding | mitigate | one open per reporter+conversation; 10 per day |
| T-05-11 | Repudiation | staff resolutions/suspensions | mitigate | audit rows 'denuncia-mensagem-resolvida' and 'mensagens-suspensas' |
</threat_model>
