## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-05-05 | Information disclosure | other people's threads | mitigate | getThread participant check → notFound; e2e with a third subscriber |
| T-05-06 | Information disclosure | expired/anonymous inbox | mitigate | requireActiveSubscription on pages and actions; inactive panel only |
| T-05-10 | Spoofing | messaging arbitrary accounts | mitigate | only origem + alvo of a visible ad/recado; recipient derived in the service |
| T-05-17 | Spoofing (CSRF) | cross-site form posts to actions | mitigate | Next server-action Origin check + src/proxy.ts foreign-Origin rejection; covered in 05-11 matrix |
